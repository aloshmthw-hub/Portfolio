ABOUT ME

All about me and my hobbies

My name is Alosh Mathew, and I am a passionate graphic designer from Kerala. I am currently pursuing my studies in animation and graphic design to enhance my skills and creativity. With a deep interest in visual arts, I focus on creating designs that are both aesthetic and functional. My love for design drives me to explore various styles, techniques, and mediums to express my ideas. Living in Kerala, I draw inspiration from its rich culture, vibrant landscapes, and diverse art forms. I believe in the power of storytelling through visuals and aim to bring my concepts to life in innovative ways. My goal is to contribute to the world of design by creating impactful and meaningful artwork that resonates with people.

I specialize in crafting visually compelling designs that effectively communicate messages and resonate with audiences. Drawing inspiration from Kerala's vibrant traditions and natural beauty, I strive to infuse local elements into contemporary design practices. Beyond academics, I actively engage in freelance projects, collaborating with diverse clients to bring their visions to life. My goal is to merge traditional aesthetics with modern design trends, creating unique and impactful visual experiences. I am committed to continuous learning and innovation, aiming to contribute meaningfully to the evolving field of graphic design.
